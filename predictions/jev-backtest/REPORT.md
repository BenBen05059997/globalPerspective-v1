## Hindsight: did the story become big? (35 runs whose following 72h is in the corpus)
A story "became big" if distinct outlets publishing the same story (same primary country and a non-frequent shared lead entity or title-token Jaccard >= 0.3) in NEW articles over the 72h after the run reach the threshold. Recall = share of became-big stories the side picked in that run or the previous 72h. Early recall counts only picks made while the story had < 4 outlets. Dead picks = picks that never got more than 2 outlets in the next 72h. Units are (run, story) pairs; GDACS picks are excluded (no outlet count).

## What changed from v3
- Combined v4: a story enters with >= 2 outlets (or GDACS); a 1-outlet story (only severity-5 ones were ever sent to Jev) enters on Jev impact >= 4, at most 2 per run. Rank = growth (distinct outlets now minus outlets of the same story already in the corpus snapshot 8h before the run), then outlet-country spread (outlet_metadata.js lists 13 of 21 candidate outlets; the rest add nothing), then outlets, then Jev impact. Drops new_since_last_brief < 0.4. GDACS Red slot, 25% category cap, must-have categories, 2-per-country cap and one pick per story as before. Rescue (1-outlet) picks: 11 over 40 runs.
- Growth baseline: top N by growth only, same 24h self-dedup.
- Fairness: ours is also deduped over 24h, and published topics with no matched candidate (94 of 547) are scored as their own picks: outlets = distinct source domains, later coverage matched with the same matcher using title tokens only (no entities). Their later coverage is probably under-counted, because LLM-written titles share fewer tokens with corpus headlines. The row "Ours, matched topics only" is the old basis; the headline row includes the unmatched topics.
- Jev answers are reused from v2. Merging changes a story's state, so some answers are missing: 25 of 1527 candidates (2%) were skipped by Jev v2 and Combined. The baseline and ours do not depend on Jev. Cached v2 spend: $0.072.
- Removed: Jev v1 column and v1_picks.json (still in git history).
- Per run: 740 articles -> 376 eligible events -> 283 clusters -> 239 merged stories -> 38 Jev-scored. 40 runs over 14 days (2026-09-30 to 2026-10-17; 10-12 to 10-15 skipped for the DeepSeek outage; 1 dropped for lack of corpus hours). Topics matched 453/547 (83%). GDACS: runs with a Red candidate 15, Orange 21.
- Selector-input sanity check (gen-1792239363975): the selector saw 182 articles; 88 (48%) are in our 733-article corpus window.

## Limitations
- Hindsight rewards stories that get widely covered, not stories that matter; a quiet consequential story scores as a miss. The outlet-count baseline is favoured by construction because "big" is defined by outlet counts.
- "Became big" uses our own classified feed and a heuristic matcher; the frequent-entity rule reduces but does not remove over-matching, and lead-entity matching can still tie unrelated stories of one actor. Outlets outside our feeds are invisible; the last runs lack a 72h window.
- The outlet-country spread bonus only sees outlets listed in outlet_metadata.js. Our feed is itself mostly Western and English-language, so the non-Western check is partial.
- Missing Jev answers after the merge change shrink the Jev v2 and Combined pools; stories with 0-1 outlets and severity < 5 were never sent to Jev, so Combined cannot rescue them.
- The classifier has no climate/science/energy/business/military category, so those quotas rarely bind. The cap of 2 per primary country is our interpretation of "regional diversity", not a stated prompt rule.
- "Ours" comes from an LLM that sees headlines plus snippets; there is no ground truth. Single backtest on about 14 days; no confidence intervals.
