MAP LEGEND · ONE VISUAL LANGUAGE FOR HOME MAP, STORY MODE AND BOARD

Each channel means one thing

SHAPE = WHAT IT IS · HUE = CRISIS TYPE · SIZE (+DOUBLE RING) = SEVERITY · BRIGHTNESS = FRESHNESS · SOLID vs DASHED = FACT vs NOT-YET / JUDGMENT · MOTION = ONLY WHAT IS NEW

1 · WHAT IT IS — SHAPE

Live situation · official feed
Diamond = an alert frame (MIL-STD / GDACS). Exact place from the feed.
News story · placed at its main country
Soft, feathered halo = approximate location (MacEachren: fuzzy reads as uncertain). Never a sharp pin.
The story you're looking at (selected / focal)
HUD target brackets (XCOM lock-on). Same mark for "selected" on the home map and the focal story in story mode.
Linked story (in story mode)
Small ringed dot at the end of an arc. Smaller than the focal story.
Country highlight · story with no exact place
Country wash only, no point at all. We don't fake a pin.
Forecast deadline · hasn't happened
Hollow + dashed = "anticipated" (MIL-STD status rule). On the time scrubber, not the map.

4 · HOW FRESH — BRIGHTNESS (USGS AGE RULE)

LIVE · <24hfull colour + glow
1–7 DAYSfull colour, no glow
OLDER · 7–30ddesaturated + "older" label
STALE · 30d+hidden from the map, counted
Old things never animate. Today every story analysis is 13 days old, so links would show in the OLDER style until DeepSeek is funded.

5 · LINKS BETWEEN STORIES — LINE STYLE

Judged to feed into · STRONG
Long dashes, thick, bright. Dashes travel from → to (deck.gl arc convention), so direction needs no arrowhead.
MEDIUM
Medium dashes, thinner, 75% opacity, slower.
WEAK
Sparse dashes, thin, 50%, no motion. Two uncertainty cues stacked (MacEachren).
Shares actors · a fact, not a cause
DOTTED grey, flatter, never moves. Different in kind from the dashed judgment links, not just fainter.
Only the selected story's arcs move. Words always sit next to the line in the card: "STRONG · model judgment".

6 · STATES — HOVER, SELECTED, SCANNED

Hover · dim the rest
Everything else drops to ~35% (Flightradar24 / MarineTraffic). The hovered marker gets a thin outline and its StoryPeek.
Selected · brackets stay
Persistence, not brightness, tells selected from hover.
Scanned by the radar
One ring flares once as the beam passes. The sweep is the one global motion; markers don't loop because of it.

MOTION BUDGET · RULES

Only three things may move: the radar sweep (one, global) · the breathing pulse on NEW/▲ markers from the last 24 h · the travelling dashes on the selected story's arcs. Nothing else.
Limits: at most 8 pulsing markers (the highest-severity ones win; the rest stay still). All cycles 1.4–4 s. Nothing flashes. Stale or older items never move.
Reduced motion (press the button above): pulses become a still glow ring, the sweep stops, and arcs keep their dash pattern without travelling. Meaning never depends on motion alone.

SOURCES OF EACH RULE
Shape by kind, solid vs dashed for "not yet" — NATO APP-6 / MIL-STD-2525. Severity steps — GDACS alert levels (but not their colours). Size steps + age-as-brightness — USGS earthquake map. Travelling dashes for direction — deck.gl arc layer / GitHub globe. Dim-the-rest on hover — Flightradar24, MarineTraffic. One global sweep + one-shot flare — XCOM 2 Geoscape. Fuzzy = approximate, stacked uncertainty cues — MacEachren. Few animated items — Harrower, "The Cognitive Limits of Animated Maps". No flashing — WCAG 2.3.
