8. "dimensions": Object scoring THIS thread's current risk across four INDEPENDENT axes. For each axis provide {"score": integer 0-100, "why": ONE sentence citing the specific development in this thread that justifies the score} — or null when the thread gives genuinely no signal for that axis. Be sparing — use null rather than a filler mid-number. A scored axis REQUIRES a cited fact in why; if the thread does not discuss an axis at all, output null for it — never a guess and never 0 for "didn't come up." Score the PRESENT situation only: a future possibility ("could become a war," "could destabilize the government") is not a severity score — that belongs in trajectory/watchQuestions, never in dimensions. Axes: "conflict" (armed violence, military operations, armed-actor intensity — war and war-risk belong ONLY here, do not also raise political for a thread that is merely about a war, sanctions, or diplomatic pressure; low = posturing/no active hostilities, moderate = localized skirmishes or a single clash with limited casualties, elevated = sustained fighting with meaningful casualties or a territorial/strategic effect, high = large-scale ongoing conflict, mass casualties, or direct state-on-state engagement), "political" (DOMESTIC institutional stability, governance, legitimacy, unrest ONLY — being at war, sanctioned, or under diplomatic/foreign pressure is NOT political risk by itself; score political only when the thread shows an actual domestic legitimacy/governance signal; low = normal governance friction, moderate = real institutional strain short of a legitimacy crisis, elevated = an actual legitimacy/governance crisis, high = institutional collapse), "economic" (financial stress, sanctions, market disruption — low = routine data/policy news, moderate = a contained shock, elevated = broad-based stress or a major sanctions/trade action, high = a systemic crisis), "humanitarian" (displacement, civilian harm, disaster — low = localized/contained hardship, moderate = a contained event with aid access functioning, elevated = large-scale displacement or aid access impaired, high = mass-casualty/mass-displacement with aid access severely restricted or a declared famine). Per-axis calibration: 0-24 = low, 25-49 = moderate, 50-74 = elevated, 75-100 = severe. (riskScore is derived from these — do NOT output it separately.)
