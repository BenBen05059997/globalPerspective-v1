How we read the world

The map is the output of a fixed pipeline, not an editor's judgement call.
What we track

Every situation on the map belongs to one of four axes — conflict, political, economic, humanitarian. A situation is created when independent outlets converge on the same event in the same place, and it stays open while coverage continues. We do not open a situation on the strength of a single report.

How severity is scored

Tiers — low, moderate, elevated, high — come from a scored combination of outlet count, geographic spread, and rate of change against the same situation's prior day. The score is deterministic: the same inputs always produce the same tier, and the inputs are shown on every situation page.

How often it updates

The feed is re-scored every 30 minutes. The stamp in the header shows the age of the data you are looking at, not the age of the page. A situation marked escalating has moved up on at least two of the three inputs since the last run.

Hue delta · your hex vs oklch-normalised
The four brand hexes are not equal-weight: humanitarian yellow reads ~0.79 L and political violet ~0.61, so yellow always looks more urgent than violet at the same tier. Normalising to oklch(0.70 0.155 h) removes the false ranking. Mock uses your hexes; this is the alternative.

Rationale
01Severity rides luminance and halo, not size: size alone fails at world zoom, and a grey ramp dies on a #0d1017 ground.
06Clustering was rejected: a hot region should look busy. Collapsing it would hide the page's core claim.
02Low tier keeps a full-saturation hue ring, so the quiet end of the scale stays legible and clickable at 9px.
07Only colliding pins get leaders; the rest stay unlabelled. Lowest tiers collapse into +N more, never the top of the stack.
03White appears only as the high-tier keyline. One exclusive channel gives high tier peripheral-vision pop with no extra colour.
08First three seconds: templated count lede, one lit callout, and the spatial spread of glow. No list reading required.
04The whole motion budget goes to escalation, so movement means one thing only and the page is still on load.
09Rail rows carry the identical pin glyph, so the map teaches the list and the list teaches the map.
05Side flags sit in empty ocean and keep every pin on its true coordinate, with straight leaders that read as instrument, not decoration.
10Detail replaces the rail rather than adding a column, so the map holds 1000px on a 13″ laptop.
